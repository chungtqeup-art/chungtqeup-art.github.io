Privacy Policy

QR Scan — QR & Barcode Scanner and Generator

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Scan ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. The same policy applies to both the iOS and Android versions of the app.

In short: QR Scan is an offline utility. We do not require an account, we do not collect or upload your personal data to our servers, and your scan/creation history is stored only on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate any backend server and we do not ask you to sign in. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, contacts, location, or the contents of the codes you scan or create. All of that stays on your device.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

The app saves a local history of the codes you scan and generate so you can view them later. This history is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete individual entries or clear the entire history at any time from within the app; uninstalling the app also removes this data.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests the following permissions, and uses them only for the stated purpose:

  • Camera — to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. The camera feed is processed on your device and is not recorded or transmitted.
  • Photo Library / Storage — to let you scan a code from an existing image, and to save QR codes you generate to your photo library.
  • Wi-Fi state — used only when you scan or create a Wi-Fi QR code, to help connect to the specified network. We do not read or transmit your saved Wi-Fi credentials.
  • Vibration — to provide haptic feedback on a successful scan.

4. Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain device information, including a mobile advertising identifier (the Google Advertising ID on Android and the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS), IP address, device type, and general usage data. This data is collected by Google, not by us.

You can learn how Google uses this information here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can limit ad personalization at any time through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (and Apple Advertising).
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Data Security

Because your history and generated codes are stored only on your device, their security depends primarily on the security of your device. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
